About me

Finance professional with a curiosity for businesses and how they create value.

I'm Abhijit Sonwane, an MBA graduate in Finance and International Trade. As an Equity Research Analyst Intern I built financial models end to end: pulling numbers from annual reports, forecasting the three statements and valuing companies with DCF and peer multiples.

I work from primary sources only, meaning NSE, BSE and company filings, on consolidated numbers. Now I want to bring that work into a finance team and grow towards investing, venture capital and venture debt.
