# abhijitsonwane.github.io

Portfolio and resume of Abhijit Sonwane. Live at https://abhijitsonwane.github.io

## How to update the site

1. Go to https://app.pagescms.org and sign in with GitHub.
2. Open this repository (`abhijitsonwane.github.io`).
3. Pick what to edit from the left menu: Research and projects, Blogs, Where I'm open to work, About photo gallery, or Your photos, resume and section photos.
4. Make your change and press **Save**. The live site updates in about a minute.

## Where things live

- `index.html`, `assets/` : the page design (no need to touch)
- `data/*.json` : the content you edit through Pages CMS
- `media/images`, `media/docs` : photos and PDFs you upload
